Human approval. Every new trade is proposed first and only executed after a person approves it with the approver PIN. The live price is re-checked at the moment of approval; if it has run more than 1% away or hit the stop, the trade is not placed. A proposal nobody decides on expires at the next scan. Stops, targets and the closing exit stay automatic because they protect capital.

How a day runs. At 09:40 IST the desk pulls live quotes for all NIFTY 500 stocks and keeps only the ones that opened at least 1.5% above the previous close, are up 2.5% or more, and are still holding above the open. The strongest names are bought (intraday) with a stop under the day’s low (2–5% away), sized so a stop-out costs 1% of capital, with no position above 20% of capital. At +1R the stop moves to breakeven; past +2R it trails 1R behind price; at +3R the target books profit. The scan repeats every 30 minutes, no new entries after 14:30, and everything is squared off by 15:20 IST, before the broker’s auto square-off. Every P&L figure is net of brokerage, STT, exchange and SEBI fees, stamp duty and GST.
